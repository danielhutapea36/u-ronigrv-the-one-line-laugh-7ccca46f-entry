A one-line laugh: the everyday truth of paying for a gym you never visit, with the surprise saved for the end — you only ever wave at the building on your way to the drive-thru.

## The line

> I pay $40 a month for a gym membership, which is a fair price for a building I wave at on my way to the drive-thru.

The joke is in the turn: the setup sounds like a complaint about a wasted membership, then the punchline reframes it — the membership is not for working out at all, it is a subscription to a building you greet in passing while driving somewhere to eat instead. Everyday, economical, and no AI or gym-bro cliché.
